      Method

      i.

      Measure

      20 pings for latency and jitter. Download and upload over 4 parallel streams for 8 seconds each, skipping the TCP warm-up. Then ping again while the line is loaded.

      ii.

      Compare

      Real 5G should land near 100 Mbps down, 20 up, 30 ms ping and 15 ms jitter. Under 40 Mbps is 4G territory, whatever the icon says.

      iii.

      Judge

      You tell us what the phone shows. We compare that label with the measured class and call it real, partial or fake.

      Where this can be wrong

      • A web page cannot read your radio type, so the 5G label is yours.
      • It measures the path to this server. A far server means lower speed and higher ping.
      • Run it a few times in different spots and at different hours. Tower load changes everything.
      • Some plans throttle on purpose. A slow result can be the plan, not the signal.
