      How the verdict works

      1. Measure

      Ping and jitter from 20 quick requests. Download and upload over 4 parallel streams for 8 seconds each, ignoring the TCP warm-up. Ping is measured again while the line is busy.

      2. Compare

      Real 5G should reach about 100 Mbps down, 20 Mbps up, 30 ms ping and 15 ms jitter. Below 40 Mbps is 4G territory, whatever the icon says.

      3. Judge

      You tell us what your phone shows. We compare the label with the measured class and call it real, partial or fake.

      Honest limits

      • A web page cannot read your radio type, so the 5G label comes from you.
      • The test measures the path from your phone to this server. A far-away server lowers speed and raises ping.
      • Run it a few times at different spots and times. Tower load changes everything.
      • Some phones and plans cap speed on purpose. A low result can be your plan, not your signal.
